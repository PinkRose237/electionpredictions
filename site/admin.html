Enter the admin password to manage election results. Publishing to the live site also needs your GitHub token, which stays in this browser.

Results desk

The public page pulls vote counts and race calls from the live feed on its own; the feed's numbers show here in grey. Type over any of them, call a race (D / R / Oth; click again to undo), or use Paste to read numbers copied from an official results site. Empty boxes keep following the feed. Overrides autosave in this browser; Publish commits them as results.json and the site picks them up within about a minute.
