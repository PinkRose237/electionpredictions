Model log

This forecast does not run on fixed settings. At every update the model is rebuilt against the latest evidence: the candidate field and nomination odds, each candidate's strength, the weight on polls versus fundamentals, the size of the uncertainty and any state-specific adjustments are re-derived, reviewed and, when the evidence calls for it, revised, inside fixed guardrails. Every revision is numbered and recorded here with its reasons.
