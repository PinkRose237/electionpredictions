How the forecast did

The final forecast, published on November 3 before any polls closed, checked against the results. Everything here updates as the last races are counted and called, so treat it as provisional until every race is in.

How this is scored

  • Only races called (two of four news organizations agree) count toward the record and Brier score.
  • Margin errors use races with at least 95% of the expected vote counted.
  • Uncontested races are left out.
