Map

Every Senate, House and governor race on one map. On election night it shows live results and race calls, refreshed every minute; switch to the forecast to see where the model expected each race to land. Hover over a state or district, or tap it, for the details.

The House map gives every district one equal square, grouped by state and placed roughly where it sits, so a dense city district counts as much as a rural one. Several states redrew their lines for 2026; square placement uses 2024 locations.

Data sources

  • Live votes and race calls: The Associated Press via The New York Times (CNN as a backup); other outlets' calls on the Race Calls page
  • Map: U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundaries and congressional district gazetteer
