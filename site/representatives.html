    Every level matters

    We look for municipal and county officials, school boards and other districts, state legislators and statewide officers, your members of Congress, and the president and vice president.

    Coverage varies by location. We show all matching records available, including offices with missing contact details, so gaps stay visible.

    Email is the first place to look

    We show public office email addresses when our sources list them. Some offices use a contact form instead of publishing an email; those forms are labeled separately.

    We never guess an email address. “Not listed in sources” means you should check the official website for another way to write.
